# FEATURES.md

## 1. Primary user
A busy person planning a meal out for a special occasion who feels overwhelmed by too many restaurant choices and wants a quick, confident recommendation.

## 2. Most important thing they should be able to do
Answer a few quick questions and instantly get a restaurant suggestion that fits their mood, occasion, budget, and distance — without endlessly scrolling reviews.

## 3–5. Feature checklist

- [ ] **User login / account** *(can wait)* — Lets users sign in so their preferences and past picks are remembered.
- [ ] **The 4-question preference quiz** *(MVP)* — Asks mood (cuisine), occasion, budget, and travel distance to capture what the user wants right now.
- [ ] **Restaurant matching engine** *(MVP)* — Takes the 4 answers and filters a list of restaurants down to the best matches.
- [ ] **Recommendation result screen** *(MVP)* — Shows the top suggested restaurant with name, photo, price range, distance, and a "why this fits you" line.
- [ ] **Map & directions link** *(can wait)* — Shows where the restaurant is and opens directions in Google/Apple Maps.
- [ ] **"Show me another option" button** *(can wait)* — Lets the user skip the first pick and see the next-best match without redoing the quiz.
- [ ] **Save favorites / history** *(can wait)* — Lets users bookmark places they liked or revisit past recommendations.
- [ ] **Reservation or call button** *(can wait)* — One tap to call the restaurant or open its booking page (e.g. OpenTable).

## MVP summary (build these 3 first)
1. The 4-question preference quiz
2. Restaurant matching engine
3. Recommendation result screen

Everything else is a nice-to-have for version 2.
